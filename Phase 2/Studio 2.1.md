**project title ** visual studio 2.1
**Prompt A** Create an artwork that uses HB graphite pencil and emphacizes curved lines that outline the trunk of a tree and organic shapes that outlines the leaves of the tree. Contrast the darker shades of the leaves with the lighter shades of the leaves. Use positive space for the tree shadow and negative space around the shadow.  Show motion of the tree as the wind is blowing through it  by blending the graphite pencil. Create texture of the tree leaves by cross-hatching. Apply pressure of the pencil to create emphasis of the tree branches. Apply balance to each side of the picture
